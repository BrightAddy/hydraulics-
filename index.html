How it works

Inside a hydraulic system.

Every circuit follows the same path. Select a component to see what it does and what we supply for it.

Pump

Turns mechanical power from an engine or electric motor into flow. Pressure builds when that flow meets resistance.

We supply · gear, vane & piston pumps
View pumps

Relief valve

Sets the maximum pressure in the circuit and protects every component downstream from overload.

We supply · relief, reducing & sequence valves
View pressure control

Directional valve

Routes flow to extend, retract or hold the actuator, by lever, solenoid or proportional control.

We supply · solenoid, manual & proportional valves
View valves

Cylinder

Turns fluid power back into straight-line force and motion. It is the part that lifts, pushes and clamps.

We supply · tie-rod, welded & telescopic cylinders
View cylinders

Return filter

Cleans the oil before it goes back to the tank. Contamination is a leading cause of hydraulic failure.

We supply · filters, elements & breathers
View filters

Reservoir

Stores the fluid, lets it cool, and gives air and contaminants time to settle out.

We supply · tanks, breathers & level gauges
View power units

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Ask a question
I don't have a part number. Can you still help?

Yes. Send photos, measurements and where the part sits on the machine, and we'll work out the right replacement or a matching equivalent.

Do you supply complete systems or just parts?

Both. We supply individual components as well as complete power units and assemblies built to your specification.

Can you make hoses to a specific length?

Yes. Tell us the length, bore, pressure rating and end fittings, and we'll build the assembly to match.

What if my machine has broken down?

Mark your enquiry as a breakdown in the form below so it's prioritised, and call us if you can.

Which industries do you work with?

Construction, agriculture, manufacturing, marine, mining, oil and gas, waste and transport, among others.

How do I get a quote?

Use the enquiry form, or call or email us. You'll get a clear price and lead time before anything is ordered.
